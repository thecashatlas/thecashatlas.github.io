Cash Atlas Studio

Cash Atlas Studio is the private publishing tool behind The Cash Atlas, a channel of animated explainers about how companies make money and how economies work.

What it does

  • Uploads the channel's own videos and sets their titles, descriptions and schedules.
  • Reads the channel's own analytics (views, watch time, audience retention, revenue) to plan future videos.
  • Reads and replies to comments on the channel's own videos.

It is used only by the channel's owner, for that one channel. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
