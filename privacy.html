Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Cash Atlas Studio ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of The Cash Atlas YouTube channel to manage that channel.

Data the app accesses

With the owner's permission, through Google's OAuth sign-in, the app uses these YouTube API permissions for the owner's own channel only:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to publish the channel's videos.
  • Manage the channel (youtube.force-ssl): to edit video titles and descriptions, and to read and reply to comments on the channel's videos.
  • View analytics and revenue reports (yt-analytics.readonly, yt-analytics-monetary.readonly): to measure how videos perform.

Comments read by the app may include the public display names of the people who wrote them. They are used only to decide which comments to reply to.

How data is stored

Access tokens and analytics data are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server, no database and no user accounts.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and does not use it for advertising. Video scripts and comment text may be processed by an AI writing assistant used by the owner to draft content and replies.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is also handled under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking it stops all access immediately.
